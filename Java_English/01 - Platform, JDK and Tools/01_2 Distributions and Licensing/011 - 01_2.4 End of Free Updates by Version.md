# End of free updates by version

Free Oracle JDK 17 updates ended in Oct 2024; Temurin 17 until at least Oct 2027; Temurin 21 until at least Dec 2029

Free updates under the permissive license (NFTC) for Oracle JDK 17 ended in September 2024, so versions after 17.0.12 require a paid subscription or use under the OTN license (development/personal only).

In the open-source ecosystem, the availability of free updates varies by vendor:

- **Eclipse Temurin (Java 17)**: Guarantees free updates until at least October 2027.
- **Eclipse Temurin (Java 21)**: Guarantees free updates until at least December 2029.
- **Oracle JDK 17 (Extended Support)**: Although premier support ended in September 2026, Oracle has waived the extended-support fee until September 2029 for subscribers.
