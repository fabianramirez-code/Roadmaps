# Oracle JDK vs OpenJDK, Eclipse Temurin, Amazon Corretto

# Oracle JDK vs OpenJDK — licensing (NFTC) and support differences

- **OpenJDK:** free and open source (GPLv2 + CPE), without commercial restrictions.
- **Oracle JDK:** (versions 17, 21, 25) free in production under NFTC, but only for a limited time and subject to conditions before requiring a paid license or support.

> In one sentence: **OpenJDK** is free and open permanently. **Oracle JDK** is free in production only temporarily under NFTC, after which commercial licensing applies.

# Eclipse Temurin — community reference distribution
This is the community-driven reference **OpenJDK** distribution managed by the **Eclipse Adoptium** working group under the **Eclipse Foundation**. *It is the direct successor to the AdoptOpenJDK project*. It is free, free from commercial licenses (GPLv2 with Classpath Exception), and TCK (Technology Compatibility Kit) certified, ensuring official compatibility with the Java specification.

Its reference status rests on several key pillars:

- **Widespread adoption**: Over 380 million total downloads and approximately 18% of the Java market, making it the most popular open-source distribution.
- **Neutral governance**: Backing from a coalition of leading technology companies (such as IBM, Microsoft, Red Hat and Google) prevents dependence on a single commercial vendor.
- **Enterprise quality**: All binaries undergo rigorous compatibility testing and the AQAvit quality verification suite, ensuring suitability for production environments.
- **Cross-platform support**: Offers optimized builds for Linux, Windows, macOS, AIX and Solaris on x64, ARM and RISC-V architectures, covering the main LTS versions (8, 11, 17, 21 and 25).

# Amazon Corretto — free long-term support

This is a free, cross-platform, production-ready **OpenJDK** distribution backed by **Amazon Web Services (AWS)**, offering long-term support (LTS) at no cost. Support includes quarterly security updates and performance improvements, plus urgent patches outside the regular cycle, providing stability and security for enterprise applications on **Linux, Windows and macOS**.

Current LTS versions and security-support end dates are:

- **Corretto 25 (LTS)**: Available since September 2025, supported until October 2032.
- **Corretto 21 (LTS)**: Available since September 2023, supported until October 2030.
- **Corretto 11 (LTS)**: Available since February 2019, supported until January 2032.
- **Corretto 8 (LTS)**: Available since January 2019, supported until December 2030.

Unlike other distributions that may require commercial licenses for extended support, Corretto is distributed under an open-source license (GPLv2 with CPE) and tested internally on thousands of Amazon production services, establishing its reliability as a drop-in replacement for Java SE.
